# 📊 Super Store Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
The **Super Store Sales Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It analyzes sales performance, profit, product quantity, customer segments, payment methods, and shipping details to generate meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Sales:** 1.57M
- **Total Profit:** 175K
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 22K
- **Number of Delivery Days:** 4 Days

## 📈 Dashboard Charts & Analysis

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

1. **Sales by Segment** – Analyzes sales across different customer segments.
2. **Sales by Payment Mode** – Shows sales distribution by payment methods.
3. **Sales by Region** – Compares sales performance across different regions.
4. **Sales by Category** – Analyzes sales across product categories.
5. **Sales by Sub-Category** – Identifies sales performance across product sub-categories.
6. **Sales by Month** – Tracks monthly sales trends.
7. **Profit by Month** – Analyzes monthly profit trends.
8. **Sales by Ship Mode** – Compares sales across different shipping methods.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Data Analysis** – Sales and profit performance analysis
- **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, graphs, and KPI cards

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
- Analyze customer segments and payment preferences.
- Compare sales across regions and product categories.
- Identify monthly sales and profit trends.
- Evaluate sales performance by shipping method.
- Present business insights through an interactive dashboard.

## 💡 Key Insights

This dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance by combining key metrics with interactive visualizations. It helps users understand sales trends, product performance, regional contributions, customer segments, and shipping preferences.
